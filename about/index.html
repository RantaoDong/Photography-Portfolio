About Me

Hi, I’m Rantao Dong

I’m Rantao Dong, currently based in Stockholm. Photography has been a long-term personal practice for me, especially as a way of observing places, people, performances, and everyday situations.

My photographs range from urban and landscape photography to events and live performances. I am particularly interested in composition, light, spatial relationships, and the small visual details that shape how a scene is perceived.

Outside photography, I work and study in areas related to computing, artificial intelligence, and interactive systems. I am also interested in film, contemporary art, and the relationship between technology and visual culture.
